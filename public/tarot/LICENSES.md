# Rider–Waite Tarot Card Images

These 22 Major Arcana images are from the Rider–Waite–Smith Tarot deck illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith and first published in 1909 under the direction of Arthur Edward Waite.

Wikimedia Commons identifies the original 1909 deck as public domain in the United States and its country of origin:
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_00_Fool.jpg
